# Progress report — 2026-10-10

## Project status
The project remains focused on a phenotype-agnostic, Zoonomia-scale screen for coordinated gene co-elimination across placental mammals. The motivating positive-control case remains the known left-right asymmetry module involving CIROP/DAND5 and related genes, but the primary goal is broader discovery of candidate co-elimination modules rather than a single-gene follow-up.

## Completed work
- Confirmed and documented the official Zoonomia species roster and assembly metadata.
- Matched the official Zoonomia taxon list and assemblies against the TOGA catalog using exact-accession QC rules.
- Built a transparent assembly selection and exclusion workflow to prevent mixing incompatible assembly versions.
- Verified that the selected TOGA status files are present and accessible for the validated species set.
- Built the gene-status matrix from the verified TOGA gene-loss summaries.
- Ran an exploratory raw shared-loss screen across the validated candidate set.
- Ran phylogenetically informed validation on the ranked candidate pairs.
- Saved the key outputs, manifests, and review artifacts in reproducible project directories.

## Verified current dataset state
- Validated Zoonomia metadata is present in `raw_data/zoonomia/`.
- Validated TOGA gene-status summaries are present in `raw_data/toga_gene_status/`.
- The gene-status matrix is available at `analysis/gene_status_matrix.tsv`.
- The ranked co-loss candidate list is available at `results/agentic_triage/ranked_co_loss_candidates.tsv`.
- Phylogenetic validation outputs are available in `results/phylogenetic_validation/` and `results/phylogenetic_validation_top10/`.

## Current interpretation
The analysis is now in the candidate-discovery and exploratory validation stage rather than the final biological-interpretation stage. The ranked list is a useful hypothesis-generation output and should be interpreted as a strong shortlist of candidate modules rather than final proof of mechanism.

## Immediate next steps
- Review the top-ranked candidate modules and their biological context.
- Interpret the strongest hits in light of known pathway and developmental biology.
- Prioritize a small set of candidate gene pairs for CRISPR or follow-up validation.
- Keep the statistical caveats explicit: the current p-values are post-selection and exploratory.
