# Session summary

Pipeline: phenotype-blind co-elimination screen across 162 mammals (TOGA calls).

## Key result
Blind screen independently recovered CIROZ (published DAND5/CIROP/CIROZ/PKD1L1/MMP21
left-right module). Other 4 members correctly excluded: DAND5/CIROP/MMP21 intact in
~70-75% of mammals (no power); PKD1L1 buried in uncertain TOGA calls (71 UL).

## Pairwise co-loss test within the left-right module
One-sided Fisher exact tests on lost ("L") status across 162 species
(`notebooks/Project1.ipynb`, last cell; table in `results/lr_module_pairwise_fisher.tsv`).
All 10 pairs show significant co-loss enrichment (p from 4.5e-22 to 3.6e-5).
Strongest: CIROP–PKD1L1 (25 species lost both, OR ~271), CIROP–MMP21 (OR ~333), DAND5–CIROP.
Pairs with CIROZ have OR = inf (CIROP–CIROZ, CIROZ–MMP21) because one cell of the 2x2 table is zero.
CIROZ is lost in 91 species (`results/CIROZ_lost_species.txt`), many of them bats, cetaceans,
carnivorans and ruminants, i.e. the losses look clade-clustered (answers part of open question 1).
Caveat: species are not independent (shared ancestry), so these p-values are exploratory;
the phylogenetic (Pagel) validation is the proper test.
